# Phase 5 — Communication Workforce

**Status:** Not accepted. Maturity per [IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md](../IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md); completion is governed by [PHASE_ACCEPTANCE_STANDARD.md](../PHASE_ACCEPTANCE_STANDARD.md).

## Objective
Introduce selected communication agents.

## Required deliverables
- Start with a small number of agents, not all eight simultaneously.
- Initial group: Receptionist, Interviewer, Scheduler and Customer Support.
- Each agent operates only within its explicitly granted permissions.

## Dependencies
Phases 3 and 4; may proceed in parallel with Phase 6.

## Related business pilot stage
Pilot Phase 4 in the [OpenWorks pilot plan](../strategy/DELANMAR_OPENWORKS_PILOT_SETUP.md) (business track; numbering is not equivalent).

## Acceptance
All deliverables above meet the applicable maturity level under the acceptance standard, with evidence, negative tests for authorization where relevant, and human-owner approval.

## Governing rule
No agent may exercise authority that the Rust Core has not explicitly granted.
