# Phase 3 — Executive & Operations Layer

**Status:** Not accepted. Maturity per [IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md](../IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md); completion is governed by [PHASE_ACCEPTANCE_STANDARD.md](../PHASE_ACCEPTANCE_STANDARD.md).

## Objective
Build the Executive Assistant and operational coordination.

## Required deliverables
- Executive Personal Assistant.
- Business Operations Director as a coordinating role, not another agent.
- Executive reporting.
- Document creation and correspondence.
- Business records and controlled access.

## Dependencies
Phases 1 and 2.

## Related business pilot stage
Pilot Phase 2 in the [OpenWorks pilot plan](../strategy/DELANMAR_OPENWORKS_PILOT_SETUP.md) (business track; numbering is not equivalent).

## Acceptance
- **Minimum maturity: Tested** for the selected executive and coordination workflows.
- Demonstrate assistant reporting, document creation and correspondence, controlled business records, coordination and escalation to the human owner.
- Test permitted actions and record access; deny unauthorized access and restricted actions without the required human approval.
- Record revision, environment, commands or procedure, results, limitations and reviewer; update the implementation status and obtain human-owner approval.

## Governing rule
No agent may exercise authority that the Rust Core has not explicitly granted.
