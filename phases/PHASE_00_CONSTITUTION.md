# Phase 00 — Engineering Constitution

**Status:** Largely established; roadmap and phase gates await human-owner approval.

## Objective and scope
Establish the technical authority, governance, roles and execution standards for work in the existing repository.

## Existing components to reuse
- [Core Architecture v1.0](../DELANMAR_CORE_ARCHITECTURE_v1.0.md), [workforce blueprint](../strategy/DELANMAR_AI_WORKFORCE_v1.1.md) and [pilot setup](../strategy/DELANMAR_OPENWORKS_PILOT_SETUP.md).
- [Build roadmap](../BUILD_ROADMAP.md), [README](../README.md) and [implementation status](../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Required work
- Confirm document precedence, human decision rights, security principles and the 24-agent roster; the Business Operations Director is a coordinating role, not a 25th specialist.
- Approve the roadmap, phase dependencies, evidence requirements and process for recording decisions and exceptions.
- Preserve the constitution's explicit version-and-rationale requirement for changes; reconcile proposals with existing pilot rollout rather than replacing it.

## Dependencies
Existing governing documents and human-owner review. No implementation gate is implied by approval.

## Testing requirements
Review links, document references, role counts, authority and phase dependencies for consistency.

## Acceptance criteria
- The human owner approves the governing documents and execution roadmap with any open decisions recorded.
- Evidence and phase closure responsibilities are clear, and no existing prototype is described as production-ready.

## Outstanding risks
Conflicting instructions, ambiguous approval authority, or treating documentation as proof of implemented controls.

## Handoff
Phase 1 uses the approved authority and evidence standards to establish a reproducible baseline.
